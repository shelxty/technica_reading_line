Read the web with your hands

Reading Line follows your hand with your webcam. Learn the seven gestures here. It takes about a minute.

📷 Step 1: Turn on the camera

Video never leaves your computer. Hand tracking runs inside the extension.

Step 2: Try each gesture

  3. 👍

    Read out loud

    Highlight a line with two fingers, then give a thumbs up and hold it. It reads to the end of the paragraph, scrolling as it goes, and each word lights up. To stop: thumbs up again, press Esc, or wave. Thumbs up once more to continue from where you stopped.

Practice area

Reading on a screen can be tiring, especially with long lines and small text. Point two fingers at any line of this paragraph and it turns black on yellow, so your eyes always know where they are. Then give a thumbs up and the line is read out loud, with each word lighting up as you hear it. Move to the next line whenever you're ready. If something is too small, pinch, hold, and lift your hand to make the page bigger.

Prefer the keyboard?

Alt+Shift+R
Read the highlighted line, or the line under the mouse
Alt+Shift+S
