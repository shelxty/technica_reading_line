Welcome to

Wavy

Read the web with your hands. Learn each gesture step by step. It takes about two minutes.

Step 1 of 9

📷

Get ready: turn on the camera

  1. Sit about an arm's length from your screen, in good light.
  2. Click Allow camera and start, then choose Allow in Chrome's prompt.
  3. Raise your hand. A small panel in the corner shows your hand's skeleton.

🔒 Video never leaves your computer. Hand tracking runs inside the extension.

The camera is off.

☝️

Point to move the cursor

  1. Hold your hand up with your palm facing the screen.
  2. Raise only your index finger. Curl the other fingers in.
  3. Move your finger around. The arrow cursor follows it.

You'll see: an arrow with a blue "☝️ Point" badge.

Tip: you don't have to stretch to reach the screen edges. Small moves go a long way.

✌️

Two fingers to highlight

  1. Raise your index and middle fingers together, like a peace sign.
  2. Move over a line in the text below. That line turns black on yellow.
  3. Now move over the link or the button. Only that part is highlighted.

You'll see: a yellow frame with a tag saying "Line", "Link" or "Button".

Tip: lower your middle finger to go back to plain pointing.

Reading on a screen can be tiring, especially with long lines and small text. Point two fingers at any line of this paragraph and it turns black on yellow, so your eyes always know where they are. Move down one line at a time whenever you are ready.

Thumbs up to read out loud

  1. Highlight a line below with two fingers and hold still for a moment.
  2. Make a fist with your thumb pointing up, and hold it for about half a second.
  3. Listen. It reads to the end of the paragraph, scrolling as it goes, and each word lights up orange.

To stop: thumbs up again, press Esc, or wave. Thumbs up once more to continue from the same line.

Tip: change the reading speed in the extension's popup.

Wavy was made for people who find reading on a screen hard. The highlight keeps your place, the voice reads with you, and the orange word shows exactly where the voice is. You can stop at any moment and pick up again from the same line, so you never lose your place.

Pinch to click

  1. Point with one finger at the big "Click me" button below.
  2. Touch your thumb and index fingertip together, then let go quickly.
  3. The button says "Clicked!".

You'll see: a ring fills around the cursor while you pinch. Let go before it's full to click.

Tip: a fist or a thumbs up never clicks, so reading can't click by accident.

Pinch, hold and move to zoom

  1. Pinch and keep holding until the ring around the cursor is full.
  2. Move your hand up to zoom in or down to zoom out.
  3. Let go to keep that size. It goes from 50% to 300%.

You'll see: a purple badge with the zoom level, like "🔍 150%".

Tip: press Ctrl+0 (⌘+0 on a Mac) to go back to 100%.

Open palm to scroll

  1. Point at the box below, then open your hand with all fingers up.
  2. Move it slowly up or down. The text moves with your hand, like sliding a sheet of paper.
  3. Close your hand to stop. It glides a little, then stops.

You'll see: a teal "🖐️ Scroll" badge.

Tip: scrolling the wrong way for you? Turn on "Reverse scroll direction" in the popup.

Wave to pause and resume

  1. Open your hand, all fingers up.
  2. Wave side to side two or three times. The badge says "💤 Sleeping" and gestures stop.
  3. Wave again to wake Wavy up.

Why: so you can talk with your hands, drink or stretch without clicking things.

Tip: Alt+Shift+S does the same from the keyboard.

You're all set!

Wavy now works on every website. Here's everything on one card:

  • ☝️ One finger: move the cursor
  • ✌️ Two fingers: highlight a line, link or button
  • 👍 Thumbs up: read out loud (again to stop)
  • 🤏 Quick pinch: click
  • 🔍 Pinch, hold, up or down: zoom
  • 🖐️ Open palm up or down: scroll
  • 👋 Wave: pause or resume

Prefer the keyboard?

Alt+Shift+H
Start or stop hand tracking
Alt+Shift+R
Read the highlighted line, or the line under the mouse
Alt+Shift+S
Pause or resume gestures
Esc
Stop reading

Click Wavy's icon in the toolbar any time to start or stop it, change the reading speed, or give yourself more time for each gesture.
