Gestures

  • ☝️One finger moves the cursor
  • ✌️Two fingers highlight a line, link or button
  • 👍Thumbs up reads from the highlighted line to the end of the paragraph. Thumbs up again (or Esc) stops
  • 🖐️Open palm up or down scrolls the page
  • 🤏Pinch to click. Pinch and hold, then move up or down to zoom
  • 👋Wave to pause gestures, wave again to resume
